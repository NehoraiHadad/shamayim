## Track D inputs

The equivalences below describe the same weather at an endpoint. A whole hour with a fully weighted observation uses the observation’s fields. Those values can differ from the old floored hourly row, including when its timestamp is the same. Between samples, Track G intentionally interpolates the inputs before the field calculations.

| Track D input | Rerouting and endpoint behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Low, mid, and high cover | Start with `shown.covers`, including the satellite correction and the existing precedence for `forcedLayers`. The field still splits low cover between the low and tower lattices and applies its calibrated occupancy. On an unchanged hourly endpoint the layer derivation is the same. When the observation supplies a different cover, the field uses that cover. |
| Rain-raised cover and precipitation level | The cover calculation uses `max(liquid rain rate, snowCloudRate)` and retains Track G’s existing 0–0.2 mm/h onset and taper for additional low cover. The physical field blends low and mid precipitation using `midWeight = (1 − smoothstep(0.25, 0.30, low)) × smoothstep(0.25, 0.30, mid)` on the cover before raising. This selects exactly D’s low or mid group outside the transition bands. Within those bands the two groups rank separately and their shaft shares, deck darkening, and drawing drift ease between the two endpoints. Light precipitation and snow can supply the field’s cloud rate. Liquid shafts still require liquid rain. |
| Cloud-base temperature, relative humidity, and solar altitude | Use the shown temperature and humidity with D’s Magnus lifting condensation level and height clamps. Reported RH gives the same base for the same fresh temperature and RH. The timeline’s reported RH share eases the base toward the existing 1,500 m fallback where RH is missing. Solar altitude remains the true sun altitude at the viewed time, including in the stratus gate. |
| Cumuliform and tower form | Derive separate WMO 80–82 and 95–99 shares before blending. Their coefficients are the original 0.6 and 1.0. Tower occupancy mixes the calibrated ranks for clear, showers, and storms using those shares and preserves `Q × g²`. A pure endpoint has the same split, occupancy and shape coefficients as D for that weather class and cover. D’s centered ±30-minute code window is replaced in the world by the shared hourly and observation timeline. |
| Nimbostratus | The frontal share is WMO 61–65 multiplied by the original low/mid overlap gate. The mid precipitating share approaches one over 0–0.2 mm/h and is combined as `wetMid + (1 − wetMid) × frontalMid`. D’s pure frontal and fully precipitating mid endpoints are preserved. The onset ramp and the layer transition are the differences near the old binary gates. |
| Near-surface wind and historical steering | Use shown 10 m wind speed and direction for the stratus gate, shaft slant, and ground wind. D’s pressure-level steering still evaluates and integrates its forecast series at representative cloud heights. It uses endpoint rows as samples of that integral. It does not take the viewed state from one of those rows. On refresh, blend the outgoing and incoming displacements and velocities with the same receipt weights as weather, holding the viewed minute fixed for a held scrub. A settled nonempty series is D’s original steering integral. When there are no hourly samples, use the original observation’s wind with the D height profile and measure extrapolation from that observation’s timestamp. This replaces the Unix-epoch phase for the no-series fallback. If a refresh changes the forecast’s reference day, the receipt interpolation includes the difference in the seeded reference offsets. |
| Rain rate and steady area | The liquid rate is Track G’s blended rain rate, including its phase and drizzle fallback. Derive the steady share from D’s WMO 51–65 and 71–75 classes at each sample. Use `(1 − steady) × clamp(rate / (4 + 2 × rate), 0.08, 0.6) + steady × 0.9`. A pure endpoint gives exactly the original D formula. Retain tower-first ranking, the cumulative-area integral, footprint radius, and the terrain-relative fall calculation. |
| Fall speed, dry virga, and terrain | Derive D’s 1, 1.5, 4, 6, and 8 m/s endpoint speeds and its `humidity / 45` multiplier for reported RH below 45% and rates below 0.2 mm/h. Blend the resulting scalars. Both are exact at a pure endpoint. Apply D’s depth-limited virga and ground-relative parabolic trail after blending, with the same flight-terrain grid and eye datum used by the shadows. Use the ranked shaft share for far, near, and edge opacity. |
| Thunder | Use shown `thunderWeight` in the rain and in the lightning pulse. A fully weighted storm reading reproduces full-strength live thunder. A fractional contribution scales the pulse. The precipitation phase and the whether-the-view-is-now rule do not consult the nearest weather code. |
| Light, above-cloud lighting, veil and optics | Pass the shown `air` to D’s light and sky consumers. `dustWeight` drives aerosol extinction and `coinOf(air)` supplies `dustWeight × farFade`; these match the old clear and dusty endpoints at weights zero and one. D’s field transmission, open-deck light, mean beam, eye light, upper-layer product, and veil based on `light.clear` remain the light paths. The near-sea glint keeps D’s eye beam. The inland water path still takes the world’s solar share. |
| Shadow geometry and the life cache | D’s terrain grid, slant shadow field and per-plane clipping consume the same field as the light and rain. The life cache uses the original observation, aerosol data, forecast-arrival weight, and satellite receipt inputs in place of the display card’s `forecast.current`. A roll-forward changes neither the original observation nor the weather supplied to these consumers. |

Shaft drawing still uses D’s projection adapter and the current near-footprint and edge-marker rules. Field genus assignment, rank ordering, visibility culls, and renderer quantization retain their existing discrete geometry decisions. The weather coefficients entering those decisions use continuous interpolation and receive no hour-key or `rollForward` reseed. Snow keeps its own painter and uses a place-stable bank seed. Ground wetness uses the blended current rain rate plus the interpolated three-hour wetness history. The current hour’s rate is counted once, including when the observation is fully weighted on the hour.
